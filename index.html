Why Brisbane is different

Why the right size matters more than the biggest size.

Brisbane gets roughly 4.2 kWh per installed kilowatt every day — among the best in the country. The catch is that most of it lands while nobody is home, and feed-in tariffs have fallen to around 5c. The money is no longer in exporting. It is in using what you generate.

We size to your usage, not your roof

We read twelve months of your interval data from Energex before we quote. If your household pulls 22 kWh a day with a 4pm peak, we tell you that — and we will happily quote you a smaller system than the last mob did if the maths says so.

Built for heat, hail and salt air

Every array is modelled for shading and thermal derating across a full Brisbane year. Near the bay we specify salt-mist-rated racking as standard. Panels are laid out to survive a southeast storm front, not just to look tidy from the street.

How it works

From first call to switch-on, typically eleven days.

  1. 01

    Free assessment

    We pull your 12-month interval data, check your roof on satellite and in person, and confirm switchboard and meter suitability. Takes about 40 minutes.

  2. 02

    A quote you can read

    One page, fixed price, itemised. Panel and inverter models named, production modelled month by month, rebates already deducted. No "from" pricing, no expiring discounts.

  3. 03

    Approvals & paperwork

    We lodge your Energex connection application and handle the STC assignment. If you are in a body corporate or a heritage overlay, we deal with that too.

  4. 04

    Installation day

    One crew, one day for most homes. We protect your roof and gutters, tidy the site, and leave the switchboard labelled and photographed.

  5. 05

    Switch-on & handover

    We commission the system, set up your monitoring app, and walk you through reading it. Then we check in at 30 days and 12 months to confirm it is performing to model.

Common questions

Questions we get asked most.

If your question is not here, the full FAQ covers another eighteen — or just call and ask.

Is a battery worth it in Brisbane yet?

For a lot of households, yes — but not all of them. With the federal Cheaper Home Batteries discount taking roughly 30% off an installed battery and feed-in tariffs down near 5c, payback on a 10 kWh battery now typically lands between 7 and 10 years for a household using 20 kWh+ per day with a strong evening peak.

If you are out all day and use very little power after dark, we will tell you to hold off. We would rather sell you the right system once.

What rebates can I actually get?

Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs) come off the price of every accredited solar install up front — on a 10 kW system in Brisbane that is usually in the region of $3,000. Batteries attract the federal Cheaper Home Batteries discount, also applied at point of sale.

We assign and claim both for you. The prices we quote are always the amount you actually pay.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installs are a single day, with power off for two to four hours while we work in the switchboard. Battery installs are usually a day and a half. Commercial jobs are scheduled around your trading hours.

Do you install panels you didn't supply?

No. We warrant the whole system for ten years on workmanship, and we can only do that when we control the specification. We will happily service, repair or extend a system another company installed.

What happens if my panels underperform?

Your quote includes a modelled monthly production figure. If your system falls more than 10% short of it over any 12-month period and it is not down to shading you added or a grid fault, we come back and fix it at no charge. That is written into our contract, not a marketing line.
