Case studies

Systems we've switched on, with the numbers attached.

Every job below is real, in Brisbane, and the production figures come from the monitoring platform rather than a brochure. Savings are calculated against the household's own prior bills.

Wavell Heights · Residential

13.2 kW across two pitches of a 1950s tile roof

A family of five with ducted air-conditioning, a pool pump and two people working from home. Their previous quote put all 30 panels on the north face. We split the array east and west so generation tracks a 7am and a 5pm peak instead of dumping midday power to the grid at 5c.

  • Tile roof, extra flashing and 14 additional penetrations sealed
  • Switchboard upgraded to accommodate a 10 kW hybrid inverter
  • Battery-ready, they added 10 kWh eighteen months later

Coorparoo · Residential + storage

10 kW and a 13.5 kWh battery on a heritage-sensitive Queenslander

A character-overlay street frontage meant nothing could be visible from the road. The whole array went on the rear pitch, with the battery mounted in the under-house storage where it stays out of the afternoon sun and holds its cycle life.

  • Rear-pitch-only layout, approved without a planning objection
  • Whole-home backup, through every storm outage since
  • Self-consumption lifted from 34% to 81% after the battery

Geebung · Small commercial

25 kW on a childcare centre

Open 6am to 6pm, five days a week, with air-conditioning running most of the year. That load profile is close to ideal for solar: the array generates exactly when the building is drawing, so very little is exported at 5c.

  • Sized against actual trading hours, not annual consumption
  • 84% of generation consumed on site
  • Installed over a weekend, no disruption to the centre
