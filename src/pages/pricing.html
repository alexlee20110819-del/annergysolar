Rebates in plain English

What the government actually pays for.

Three separate schemes get confused constantly. Here is how each one works in Queensland, and which of them we handle for you.

STCs (solar)

A federal certificate scheme. On a 10 kW Brisbane system it is worth roughly $3,000, and it comes off the invoice up front — you never see a form.

We handle it. Prices on this page already include it.

Cheaper Home Batteries

The federal battery discount, worth roughly 30% off an installed battery. Applied at point of sale by an accredited installer, and it steps down each year.

We handle it. Battery prices quoted are net of the discount.

Feed-in tariff

Not a rebate — it is what your retailer pays for exported power. In South East Queensland it is market-set and currently sits near 5c/kWh. Worth shopping around for.

Your retailer sets it. We will tell you who is paying best.

Rebate values change annually and with your postcode. Figures above are indicative for Brisbane in 2026 — your quote will carry the exact amount that applies on the day.
