Battery storage

Keep your afternoon sun for the evening

Exporting at 5c and buying back at 33c is a bad trade. A battery closes that gap — and with the federal discount, the maths finally works for a lot of Brisbane households.

  • Blackout backup wired to your essential circuits, or the whole house
  • Retrofit to an existing array, including systems we did not install
  • VPP-ready if you want to join one — and no pressure if you don't
  • An honest payback figure before you commit, even when it is a long one

Commercial solar

30 kW to 500 kW, modelled on your tariff

Commercial solar lives or dies on the tariff. If you are on a demand charge, total consumption barely matters — what matters is your half-hourly peak. We model that first.

  • Warehouses, cold storage, clubs, childcare, strata and light industrial
  • Half-hourly load analysis and demand-peak shaving modelling
  • Instant asset write-off and depreciation position explained in the proposal
  • Energex negotiation for export limits and connection approvals
  • Staged installation so your operation keeps running

EV charger installation

A 7 kW single-phase or 22 kW three-phase wall charger, wired so it prefers your own solar over grid power. We install Tesla Wall Connector, Fronius Wattpilot and Zappi.
