How we spec a battery

  • Usable capacity first — sized to your evening load, typically 10 to 20 kWh
  • Matched to your inverter — hybrid or AC-coupled, whichever suits your existing setup
  • Rated for a Queensland garage — chemistry and enclosure chosen for summer heat

We only fit batteries on the Clean Energy Council approved list, so every system we quote is eligible for the federal battery discount. We are not tied to a single brand — we recommend what fits your load and your inverter.

Battery storage

Keep your afternoon sun for the evening

Exporting at 5c and buying back at 33c is a bad trade. A battery closes that gap — and with the federal discount, the maths finally works for a lot of Brisbane households.

  • Blackout backup wired to your essential circuits, or the whole house
  • Retrofit to an existing array, including systems we did not install
  • VPP-ready if you want to join one — and no pressure if you don't
  • An honest payback figure before you commit, even when it is a long one

  • Sungrow — strong hybrid range for battery-ready systems

Every panel and inverter we fit is on the Clean Energy Council approved product list, which is what makes your system eligible for the STC rebate. Your quote names the exact model going on your roof — never just “tier 1”.
