About Annergy

An electrical contractor that happens to specialise in solar.

Not a sales office. Not a franchise. Not a call centre that books subcontractors it has never met. We started on Brisbane roofs in 2013 and we are still on them.

The short version

Why we started

Annergy began because two Brisbane sparkies kept getting called out to fix solar systems that should never have been installed the way they were. Undersized cable. Isolators mounted where the sun destroys them inside three years. Arrays pointed north on houses whose owners were never home at midday.

The industry had a design problem dressed up as a price problem. Thirteen years and 2,400 systems later, our answer is still the same: read the data, size it honestly, install it properly, and answer the phone afterwards.

How we work

Four commitments we put in writing.

These are in our contract, not just on this page. If we break one of them, you have something to hold us to.

A production figure we're accountable for

Your quote carries a modelled monthly generation forecast. If the system falls more than 10% short over any twelve-month period, and it is not down to new shading or a grid fault, we return and remedy it at no cost.

Ten years on our workmanship

Separate from the manufacturer warranties on panels and inverters. It covers the part that most commonly fails on a badly installed system — the installation itself.

Fixed price, or we wear it

Everything we find at the assessment goes in the quote. If we miss something, that is our cost to absorb — not a variation invoice handed to you on install day.

No pressure selling, ever

We do not door-knock, we do not cold-call, we do not run discounts that expire at midnight, and we never sell your details to a lead broker. Quotes stay valid for 30 days at the quoted price.

The team

Twenty-two people, all on our own payroll.

Six install crews, two designers, three in the office, and a service technician whose entire job is looking after systems that are already on roofs.

Accredited designers

Every system is designed by a CEC Accredited Designer before it is quoted — not sketched by a salesperson on a tablet in your kitchen.

Licensed electricians

All install crews are led by a licensed QLD electrician holding CEC installer accreditation. Apprentices work under supervision, never alone.

A dedicated service tech

One person's whole role is aftercare: 30-day checks, twelve-month reviews, warranty claims and rescue jobs on other companies' work.
